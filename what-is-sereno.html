What is Sereno?

Sereno is a sleep sound machine app. You layer sounds such as rain, ocean, wind, streams, fire, night crickets, fans and white, pink or brown noise, and set the volume, balance and EQ of each layer to build your own mix. Nature sounds are real field recordings re-sequenced at random as you listen, so they are not a fixed loop, while colored noise is generated live on the device. There are 1000+ ready-made blends and scenes such as Rainy Night, Deep Sleep, Steady Focus, Study Session, Meditation and Red-Eye Flight.

Who is Sereno for?

People who want a steady background sound to fall asleep, and people who use brown or pink noise to focus while working, reading or studying. It also suits travellers and anyone who wants sound without a connection: playback works fully offline, keeps going in the background and on the lock screen, and there is no account.

How does Sereno charge?

Sereno is free to download. The listing states that some sounds, scenes and features are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase; there are no ads and no account. For the current price, check the App Store page, because prices differ by country.

How is Sereno different from other sleep sound apps?

Based on what the listing describes: nature sounds are real recordings that take a fresh path each play instead of repeating one loop; you can layer as many sounds as you like and tune every track; the sleep timer fades out softly instead of cutting off; a sunrise wake-up brings in birdsong at the time you choose; a full-screen night view dims the display; and there is no tracking, no ads, no account and no data collected.
