Sleep Sound App: Questions to Ask Before You Download

Looking for a sleep sound machine on your iPhone, but tired of short loops you can hear repeating at 2 a.m.? Here are the questions people ask before downloading, answered from the Sereno App Store listing.

  • What it does
  • Questions

What Sereno does

  • Dozens of living sounds: white, pink and brown noise, rain, ocean, wind, streams, fire, night crickets, fans and more.
  • Unlimited mixing: layer as many sounds as you like, with volume, balance and EQ for every track.
  • Gentle sleep tools: a sleep timer that fades out softly and a sunrise wake-up with birdsong.
  • Private by design: no tracking, no ads, no account, works fully offline.

Questions

Will I hear the same loop repeating all night?

No. Nature and ambient sounds are real field recordings, re-sequenced at random as you listen, so every play takes a fresh path. Colored noise is generated live on your device.

Can I mix rain with brown noise?

Yes. Layer as many sounds as you like and set the volume, balance and EQ of each track: thunder over a crackling fire, rain on a tent, a fan behind soft brown noise.

Does it stop abruptly when the timer ends?

No. The sleep timer fades out softly instead of cutting off. A sunrise wake-up can gently bring in birdsong at the time you choose.

Does it keep playing with the screen locked or without a connection?

Yes. Sereno plays in the background with lock-screen controls and works fully offline. A full-screen night view dims the display.

Can I use it to focus, not just sleep?

Yes. Steady brown and pink noise gives an even background for work, reading and study, with focus scenes like Steady Focus and Study Session.

Who is Sereno for?

For anyone who falls asleep better, or focuses better, with steady background sound and wants a mix that does not feel like a short loop. Some sounds, scenes and features are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase.
