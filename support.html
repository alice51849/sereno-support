Getting started

Open the Sounds tab and tap any sound to start playing. Tap more sounds to layer them, then open the Mixer to set each track's volume, balance and EQ. Save a mix you love as your own scene.

Scenes

The Scenes tab has ready-made mixes for sleep, focus, ADHD focus, study, relaxation, baby sleep and more. Tap one to play it instantly.

Sleep timer & sunrise

In the Sleep tab, choose a timer duration to gently fade the sound out as you drift off. Turn on Sunrise wake-up and pick a time to wake gently to rising birdsong while Sereno plays through the night.

Background & lock screen

Sereno keeps playing when your screen is off and can be controlled from the lock screen and Control Center, as well as over AirPlay.

Sereno Pro

Sereno Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks every sound, unlimited mixing layers, all scenes, tuning controls, all themes and the immersive mode — with no subscription. Purchased on another device? Tap "Restore Purchases" on the paywall or in Settings.

Works offline

Sereno needs no internet connection. All sound is generated on your device, and no data is collected.
