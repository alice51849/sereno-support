Sereno: Sleep Sounds · Support

Close and reopen the app, confirm that the latest App Store version is installed, and include the device model, iOS version, app version and steps to reproduce when emailing support.

What the app does

Mix calm soundscapes for sleep, focus and relaxation. Playback, mixing and timers work offline without looping ads or an account.

Troubleshooting

Close and reopen the app, confirm that the latest App Store version is installed, and include the device model, iOS version, app version and steps to reproduce when emailing support.

Data and privacy

Sound choices, mixes, timers and settings stay on the device. There is no account, third-party advertising, tracking or third-party analytics. No account is required. The app contains no third-party advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics.

Purchase

Sereno is free to try. An optional one-time lifetime purchase unlocks the full sound library; Apple handles the purchase and there is no subscription.

Deletion and recovery

Delete records from inside the app when that control is available. Deleting the app removes its local container; Apple-managed backups remain governed by your device settings.
